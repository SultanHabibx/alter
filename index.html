Alter — Support

Minimalist budget tracker for iPhone and Android

Need help, found a bug, or have a suggestion? Email sultanhabibislam@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Alter has no accounts and no servers — nothing you enter is uploaded anywhere.

How do I back up my data or move it to a new phone?

Open Settings → Backup & restore → Back up data and save the file somewhere safe, such as Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email. On the new phone, install Alter and choose Restore from backup. Backups work between iPhone and Android.

How do I export my transactions to Excel?

Open Settings → Reports → Export to Excel. Alter creates a spreadsheet with a summary, your expenses and your income, and opens it in Excel, Numbers, Google Sheets or any spreadsheet app you have.

How do asset prices work?

In Assets on the Home screen, add gold (by weight and karat), silver, crypto, stocks or any other asset. Alter updates prices automatically when you open the app and every minute while the Assets page is open: gold and silver from gold-api.com, crypto from Coinbase and stocks from Yahoo Finance, converted to your currency. Your holdings stay on your phone — only the symbols being priced are sent. Without internet, Alter shows the last prices it downloaded.

How do price alerts work?

Open an asset in Assets and tap Add alert: notify me when the price goes above or below a level, or rises or drops by a percentage. Alerts are checked whenever you open Alter, and in the background as often as your phone allows, which on iPhone depends on how often you use the app. Each alert fires once; you can turn it on again from the same screen.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your phone. Because everything is stored only on your device, this permanently removes all of your data. Backup files you saved yourself are not affected.

Does Alter work offline?

Yes. Everything works offline; an internet connection is only used to update asset prices.
