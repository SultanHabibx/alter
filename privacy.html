Alter — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

The short version

Alter stores all of your data — transactions, budgets, recurring items, and settings — on your own device. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any of your personal or financial information.

Data storage and deletion

All information you enter into Alter is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers — Alter has none. Deleting the app permanently deletes all of this data from your device. You can export your transactions at any time as an Excel file.

Backups

When you tap "Back up data", Alter creates a backup file and hands it to the app or location you choose (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email). Alter never uploads it anywhere by itself. Your phone's own system backup (iCloud Backup or Google backup) may also include Alter's data, depending on your device settings; these backups are managed by Apple or Google, not by us.

Analytics and tracking

Alter contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Network access

Alter works offline. The only internet requests it makes are to download market prices for the Assets section: gold-api.com (gold and silver), Coinbase (crypto prices and exchange rates), currency-api via jsDelivr and Cloudflare (exchange rates) and Yahoo Finance (stocks). These requests contain only the symbols being priced, such as XAU, BTC or 2222.SR, and never your amounts, balances, transactions or any personal information. Prices are only requested when you have assets or open the Assets section, and, if you set price alerts, during short background checks that your phone schedules. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address; their own privacy policies apply.

Permissions

Alter does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other sensitive device features. If you create a price alert, Alter asks for permission to send you notifications; you can turn this off at any time in your phone's settings. When you restore a backup, you choose the file yourself, and Alter reads only that file.

Children's privacy

Alter does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
